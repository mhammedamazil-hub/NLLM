Local storage & privacy

Files are processed in your browser. No application server or account.

On this deviceNotebooks, imported text, conversations and indexes use browser IndexedDB. You can export a JSON backup or delete notebooks at any time.
When you use AI chatThe configured provider receives your question and retrieved source excerpts. Their terms, billing, retention and CORS rules apply. No request occurs until you submit one.
OfflineThe installed app shell and saved notebooks open offline. AI generation needs internet and a compatible provider; this app does not run a local AI model.
Website & media importsArbitrary web pages are not scraped; no CORS proxy is used. PDF, DOCX, and PPTX text are extracted locally. Scanned-PDF OCR, audio, and video parsing are not supported.
